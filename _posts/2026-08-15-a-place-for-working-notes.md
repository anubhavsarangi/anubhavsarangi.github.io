Most useful work begins untidily. A question appears, evidence gathers around it, and slowly a shape becomes visible. The finished diagram rarely shows that part.

This site is for that missing middle.

I want a place to write about building software, data systems, and applied AI without pretending every idea is a grand framework. Some posts will be careful technical notes. Others will be travel memories, unfinished observations, or lessons that took longer than they should have.

The common thread is attention: to how systems actually behave, to what the evidence supports, and to details that disappear if they are not written down.

The site itself follows the same principle. It is deliberately small. The pages are plain HTML and CSS; the writing is Markdown. There is very little here to maintain, which leaves more room for the part that matters: publishing something worth reading.
